Hackathon Architectural Defense Model

CyberShield is engineered using a decoupled worker-thread architecture. Incoming login events stream into an asynchronous, thread-safe queue.Queue.

Concurrency & Thread Safety: Per-account state transitions are protected with an internal threading.RLock, guaranteeing atomic counter increments and eliminating lost writes even when dozens of threads target the same account concurrently.

Out-of-Order Resiliency: Sliding time windows sort events chronologically by parsed UTC timestamps rather than arrival sequence, preventing evasion by replayed or delayed packets.

Defensive Simulation Notice: Built strictly for defensive educational simulations. Does not capture or store credentials or real passwords.
